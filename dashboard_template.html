How to update

This page can't run the model by itself, on purpose: it has no access to your computer or the internet. To refresh it:

  1. Made transfers? Update my_team.txt (players, bank, free transfers, selling prices).
  2. Double-click update.command (Mac) or update.bat (Windows). It downloads fresh data, reruns the model and reopens this page.
  3. Or run this in a terminal, inside the model folder:

Best times: once after the gameweek ends (results and prices), and again a few hours before the deadline (team news).

How the model works

Minutes. Recent minutes (last 6 gameweeks, newest weighted most) give the chance of playing and of playing 60+, times FPL's injury flag.

Rates. Per-90 xG, xA, saves, defensive actions, bonus and cards, blended with last season so a few hot games don't dominate.

Fixture. Each team's expected goals in this match, from attack and defence ratings plus home advantage. Clean-sheet chance comes from the opponent's expected goals.

Points. All of that priced with current FPL scoring, then simulated 20,000 times for the floor, ceiling and haul chance.

Picks. An optimiser chooses the 15, the XI and the captain for the next five gameweeks within budget, formation and the 3-per-club rule.

One gameweek is mostly luck. Treat gaps under 0.5 xP as a coin flip and check team news before the deadline.
